About Care Awaits

Care Awaits is a disability support provider based in Brisbane. We match each client with a support worker who suits their needs, routines and goals.

Our directors, Darcy and Trinity, started Care Awaits so that people can live life on their own terms, with care that is respectful, dependable and personal.

As we grow, we are building a team of dedicated support workers across Brisbane and surrounding areas, so that more people can have support they trust.

Trained, screened and ready to care

You should always feel safe with the person supporting you. Every Care Awaits support worker is trained and checked before they work with you.

  • First Aid and CPR

    Current certification, so help is at hand if it is ever needed.

  • Blue Card

    Our support workers hold a current Blue Card.

  • Worker screening checks

    Required screening is completed before anyone starts work with you.

Our services

Support is shaped around you. Tell us what matters, and we will build a plan with the right support worker.

  • Disability support work

    Skilled support workers matched to your needs, preferences and goals, whether you need regular help or support for particular tasks.

  • Personal care

    Respectful help with everyday personal care, delivered with privacy, dignity and your choices at the centre.

  • In-home and daily living support

    Practical help around the home, from meals and household tasks to routines, so you can stay comfortable and independent.

  • Community and social participation

    Support to get out and about, join activities, see friends and stay connected to your community.

  • Transport and travel assistance

    Reliable support to get to appointments, work, activities and outings, with a support worker by your side.

  • Skills development and capacity building

    Hands-on support to build confidence and independence, and to work towards the goals you set for yourself.

Support that goes where life goes.

At home, out in the community, or on the way to what matters, we are by your side.
